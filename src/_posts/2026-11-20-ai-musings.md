AI has become a blessing and a curse for most employed humans.

#### The pros tend to be:
- Can use it to accomplish tasks in minutes instead of hours or days.
- Can wire it up in such a way it can run your digital life.
- Can vibe code an entire application to solve everyday problems, or to launch your next big SaaS idea.
- Can slop up a banner or flyer for your social media post.
- Frees you up to more exciting or relaxing things.
- Anyone can be a Software Developer, a Project Manager, a Communications Director, a Digital Artist. Just keep prompting until it looks right.

#### The cons tend to be:
- Dumbs you down and doesn't help you learn at your pace. Spoon feeds you what you want to hear and see.
- Sometimes takes unethical or untested shortcuts to deliver results.
- Gives you the illusion you are making headway and progress, but you never seem to chip away at that backlog of tasks. Or get ahead of the firehose of next actions or "one more thing..." requests (because everyone around you is also using AI!).
- Keeping up with the hypeware aspect. New tools, new models, new workflows, new ways to accomplish everything and nothing at the same time.

### My take?

"Stradle the Fence". Try it out, and try to do the exact same things without using it. Let it help you, but don't give it everything. Ask it to build the tools and programs you can use offline to do the tasks it's burning tokens to please you with. Always dodge the proverbial bus. The double edge sword of AI can smite your "enemies" just as easy as it can cut your hand off. 

And for the love of everything, pay the humans first, the bots later.
